Master’s research

Leakage-Controlled Outcome Prediction and Precedent Retrieval for Amharic Cassation Decisions of the Ethiopian Federal Supreme Court

MSc thesis · Addis Ababa University · October 2026

My thesis investigated how evaluation leakage affects Amharic legal outcome prediction, alongside lexical and dense methods for retrieving relevant precedents from Ethiopian Federal Supreme Court Cassation decisions.

  • Curated and audited 7,460 decisions; residual bench reasoning remained in 73.0% of statistically trimmed cases.
  • Evaluated AfroXLMR-large and EthioLLM-large using continued pretraining, tokenizer adaptation, and long-document representations, with representation changes analyzed using CKA.
  • Compared Transformer encoders with a FastText-BiGRU-Att baseline across leakage-control settings, using multi-seed evaluations, chronological splits, and label checks.
  • Developed lexical and dense precedent retrieval using BM25/BGE-M3, weighted reciprocal rank fusion, and reranking, achieving 85.8% pool-based Recall@100 against silver relevance judgments.

Thesis advisor: Dr. Martha Yifiru Tachbelie. Research fellowship at the Resonance AI4D Lab under Dr. Fitsum Assamnew Andargie.
